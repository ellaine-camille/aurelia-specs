**Purpose:** preserve incidents once they are observed and reconstructed.

**Confidence note:** no application incidents are documented or reconstructable from the initial commits on 2026-11-22.

## Incidents

No incidents are recorded.

## Required record format

For each future incident:

1. What happened and which user-visible operation failed.
2. Why it happened, including the invariant or coupling that broke.
3. How it was found and what exact signal identified it.
4. The fix and release/rollback state.
5. The lesson and the lockstep set, playbook step, or principle it updates.
6. Confidence: verified, inferred, or suspected.
